On Linux the folders follow XDG_DATA_HOME, XDG_CONFIG_HOME, XDG_STATE_HOME and XDG_CACHE_HOME when set. TIFFIN_HOME puts everything in one folder instead.

Inside the data folder, tools/ holds installed packages, services/ the databases' data, mail/ caught mail and ca/ Tiffin's certificate authority. tiffin db backup writes to backups/.

Ports ​

All of them on loopback only (127.0.0.1, ::1); nothing listens on your network unless you share a site with tiffin share --lan.

PortWhat
80, 443 (else 8080, 8443)your sites, HTTP and HTTPS
53 on 127.0.53.1 (Windows, Linux), 1053 (macOS).test names
2525caught mail (SMTP)
9913dumps and queries from the Laravel package
each service's owndatabases and caches: tiffin service list

.test names, mail and dumps move with tiffin config set or the app's Settings > Ports (saved in settings.json in the settings folder); any of them with Environment variables, which win over a saved address.
